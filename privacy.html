Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2024

Introduction

We take the protection of your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how the QU QR Reader mobile application ("Application") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Important: QU QR Reader works completely offline. All your data is stored locally on the device and is never transmitted to our servers.

1. What Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

The application may process the following information:

  • QR Code Content: text, URLs, contact information that you scan or create
  • Scan History: records of previously scanned codes
  • User Settings: your preferences for working with the application

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

The application does NOT collect automatically:

  • Usage data and analytics
  • Device information
  • IP address or location
  • Device identifiers

2. Application Permissions

For proper operation, the Application requests the following permissions:

Camera Access

Required for scanning QR codes and barcodes

Photo Library Access

For saving generated QR codes to gallery (optional)

Contacts Access

For adding contacts from scanned vCards (optional)

Location Access

For creating QR codes with current location (optional)

3. How We Use Information

All information is used exclusively for:

Core Functions

Scanning and creating QR codes

Scan History

Saving history for quick access

Personalization

Saving your settings

Performance Improvement

Performance optimization

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All data is stored exclusively on your device in a secure local database. We do not have access to this information.

Security Measures:

  • Encryption: Local data is protected by iOS system encryption
  • Isolation: Data is accessible only to your application
  • No Transmission: Information never leaves your device
  • Control: You can delete all data at any time

5. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We DO NOT share your data

QU QR Reader does not share, sell, or transfer your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

Exceptions: Data transfer is possible only:

  • At your direct request (for example, sharing a QR code)
  • When using iOS system functions (contacts, gallery)
  • As required by law (only upon official request)

6. Your Rights and Control

Right to Access

You can view all saved data in the "History" section

Right to Modify

You can edit or delete individual entries

Right to Delete

Complete deletion of all data through application settings

Right to Export

Ability to export your QR codes in various formats

7. Cookies and Tracking

Since QU QR Reader is a mobile application that works offline, we do not use:

  • Cookies or web beacons
  • Analytics systems (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • Advertising trackers
  • Social plugins

8. Children and Privacy

The application is suitable for users of all ages. We do not collect personal information from children under 13 years old. If you are a parent and discovered that your child is using the application, you can delete all data at any time.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Significant changes will be communicated through:

  • Application update in the App Store
  • Notification within the application itself
  • Update on this website
